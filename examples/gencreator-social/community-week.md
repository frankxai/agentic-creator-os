# GenCreator community week

Proposed week: 12–18 October 2026, Europe/Amsterdam. Every item is an unsent draft.
The three audience tracks share one content workflow; each keeps its own source
and editorial decisions. Use one source edition at a time until creator capacity
and account readiness are known.

## Editorial choices

| Track | Source and finished drafts | Reader benefit | Review before handoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AI builders and independent creators | [builders.json](builders.json): LinkedIn explanation, newsletter, short demonstration script | Recover an editable draft after the chat closes | Verify the workflow claims against an actual packet; show recovery without fabricated benchmark savings |
| Artists, musicians and storytellers | [artists.json](artists.json): six-panel story, newsletter exercise, narrated script | Adapt an original scene without losing its physical details or ending | Preserve the standalone fiction label; inspect panel readability; audio and artwork need their own rights |
| Businesses and client teams | [teams.json](teams.json): professional explanation, newsletter, demonstration script | Keep source, draft revisions and review responsibility clear | Retain the hypothetical scenario label; obtain real client facts before turning it into a case study |

## Work to do this week

Monday: verify all three source files and inspect the nine editable drafts.
Select the first track with a real owner and known capacity. Record that choice
in the existing Mission. Keep the other tracks ready without claiming accounts,
approval or a booked publishing time.

Tuesday: revise its post or carousel for that brand's actual audience. Keep the
newsletter useful on its own: include the complete explanation or exercise,
not merely a link to the post. Review source support and remove unsupported claims.

Wednesday: make the demonstration or narrated video from the finished script.
The script alone is not a video. Supply original or licensed media, inspect
captions and readable framing, and write a real asset receipt. If production is
unavailable, preserve the script and continue with the reviewed text deliverable.

Thursday: prepare an unsent packet for each exact destination the creator chooses.
Recheck the complete revision after edits. A verified export gives the creator
files they can inspect; posting remains a separate authorized action.

Friday: if anything was actually delivered, record supplied native observations
with account, format, audience, age and metric definitions. If delivery or
observations are missing, record that state. Keep the next experiment pending.

Weekend: recover one packet from files without chat memory. Edit its edition JSON
and prepare a new revision. Preserve the original and compare the repair steps
with the creator's current host-and-files workflow.

in the narrated treatment. No established fictional universe is implied.

Teams: the source is a hypothetical operations example. Replace it with an
