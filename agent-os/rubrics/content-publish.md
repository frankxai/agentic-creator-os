# Rubric: content and publishing packages

Score the package as the audience will meet it, per channel.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Claims | Unsupported result, income, or performance claims | Claims without sources | Every claim sourced or labelled opinion | Sourced, specific, and modest |
| Voice and register | Wrong register or generic AI voice | Neutral | On voice | Recognisably the creator, per the identity files |
| Hook and structure | Buried point | Clear but slow | Strong first line; scannable | Earns the next line every line |
| Channel fit | Wrong length or format | Adapted superficially | Native to the channel | Native, with the channel's best-performing structure |
| Visual pairing | No asset or mismatched | Asset present, no alt | Asset fits, alt written | Asset carries the idea; alt and Open Graph verified rendered |
| Links and calls to action | Broken links | Works, unclear action | Clear action, links checked | One clear action, tracked |
| Disclosure | Synthetic media or sponsorship undisclosed | Partial | Disclosed where required | Disclosed plainly, no hedging |
| Timing | No schedule | Arbitrary time | Proposed for the audience | Proposed with the reason and the metric to watch |

**Ship bar:** no 0s, claims and disclosure at 2 or above, average of at least 2.2. Publishing itself is always the owner's button.
