# Rubric: art and media assets

Score each candidate 0–3 after looking at it. Never score an image or a video
frame you have not opened.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Brief fit | Does not serve the stated job or placement | Loosely related | Serves the job | Serves the job and the placement's crop and context |
| Composition | Confused focal point | Centered default | Intentional framing | Framing that carries the central idea |
| Craft and detail | Visible artifacts (hands, faces, text, seams) | Minor artifacts | Clean at intended size | Clean at full resolution; holds up on close look |
| Brand register and palette | Wrong register | Generic AI look | On register | Unmistakably this brand's visual world |
| Originality | Imitates a known work or style closely | Common motif | Distinct | A motif worth reusing across the brand |
| Consistency | Characters or products drift | Noticeable drift | Consistent within the set | Consistent with references across sets |
| Text in image | Misspelled or garbled | Readable but off-brand | Correct | Correct and typographically intentional; or no essential text baked in |
| Accessibility | No alt intent | Alt intent vague | Alt intent clear; essential text also in the page | Also reduced-motion and caption plan for video |
| Provenance and rights | Unknown source or third-party marks | Source noted, rights unclear | Original generation with prompt and model recorded | Also cleared for the destination and channel |

**Ship bar for "candidate → reviewed":**
- no 0s and an average of at least 2.2
- provenance at 2 or above
- the asset record complete (job, placement, model, prompt, rights note, alt intent)

A candidate never counts as released until it is integrated in its destination and verified in the rendered page.
